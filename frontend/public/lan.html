Open Lisan on your phone

Camera and microphone only work on https:// after you trust the Lisan certificate on this device.

Do this first on the phone
  1. Stay on the same Wi‑Fi / hotspot as the PC.
  2. Tap Download certificate below and install it.
  3. iPhone: Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install → then General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → enable Lisan.
  4. Android: open the downloaded .crt → install as CA / VPN & apps (wording varies).
  5. Come back and tap Open Lisan.
